## How a claim becomes publishable

1. Identify the exact game, preferably with its place or universe ID.
2. Capture the source, check date, and version scope.
3. Distinguish direct evidence from a player's report or our suggested strategy.
4. Publish only the part that the evidence supports.
5. Recheck after a relevant update or correction.

We prefer the official Roblox record, developer announcements, and exact-game observations. Gameplay videos can support a visible interface detail only after we identify the experience and inspect the relevant moment. Player reports stay labeled until corroborated.

## Our current limitations

We have not completed an in-game test for this launch. Search results, competing wikis, and video titles were used to find research leads, not to establish code rewards or numeric gameplay rules. Restricted community channels were not audited.

A missing code in our records means **not verified**, not “proved impossible.” A metadata update time means **updated record**, not a verified patch changelog.
